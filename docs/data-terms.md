# Fixture data terms

The CSV and sidecar files under `fixtures/` are original teaching data written for MachineSight. They were typed as exact tables. There is no random seed, no imported archive, and no measurement from a shop, a vehicle, or a person.

You may copy them under the MIT license in `LICENSE`. They do not show that any real machine agreed or disagreed with a sensor.

Do not add a field capture to this repository unless the commit also records where it came from, who is allowed to distribute it, the instrument calibration status, and the time base. Logs that contain names, contact details, hostnames, or credentials do not belong here.

Checksums are listed in `fixtures/MANIFEST.md`.
